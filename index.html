# What is this

Infinity Explainer replaces the chat thread with an infinite canvas. Every answer is a card. Select any words inside a card, ask about them, and a new card grows from exactly that spot, joined by a thin line. Pan around, zoom out to see how your questions branch, zoom in to keep reading.

# Why

In a normal chat, one confusing word means a follow-up message, and the answer lands at the bottom, far from what you were reading. The context scrolls away. Here, each explanation stays beside the words that raised it, so the path through an idea stays visible.
